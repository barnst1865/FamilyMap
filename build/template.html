    About the data

    Koik, Leoke and Rodilitz–Zaslow places come from the family research of October 2026 (parish registers, revisions, passenger lists, censuses). Goundrey and Romegialli places come from the Ancestry tree export of 31 October 2026 and have mostly not been checked yet.

    Where a year or place is only roughly known, the dot is hollow. Farm sites around Viljandi and Halliste are placed approximately. Moves between records are drawn as straight journeys at the time of the next record, so a move may have happened earlier than shown. Some residences are inferred from where a child was born.

    Generations are counted from the youngest generation, where all four branches meet. Living people are not shown.
